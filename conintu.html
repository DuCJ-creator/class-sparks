高階英文核心字彙：破解學術與大考的隱喻盲點

💡 提示：點擊下方標籤瀏覽單字核心概念，按 🔊 聽發音，接著完成下方的實戰挑戰題吧！

🚗
vehicle
直覺原意：車輛、汽車
抽象轉義：媒介、手法、工具、載體
搭配詞：art is a vehicle for emotion / vehicle of expression
🪟
window
直覺原意：窗戶
抽象轉義：短暫的時機、機會之窗、時間限制
搭配詞：a narrow window of time / a window of opportunity
🏠
house
直覺原意：房子
抽象轉義：收藏、容納、提供空間 (動詞)
搭配詞：the library houses rare manuscripts.
🌾
field
直覺原意：田地、球場
抽象轉義：專業領域、學術學門
搭配詞：an expert in the field of AI
🚪
room
直覺原意：房間
抽象轉義：空間、餘地、進步空間 (不可數)
搭配詞：there is room for improvement.
🔝
ceiling
直覺原意：天花板
抽象轉義：最高限度、無法逾越的障礙
搭配詞：hit a glass ceiling / price ceiling
📡
channel
直覺原意：電視頻道、海峽
抽象轉義：管道、途徑 (溝通或資源)
搭配詞：complaints through official channels
🛣️
avenue
直覺原意：大道、馬路
抽象轉義：途徑、手段 (解決問題的方法)
搭配詞：explore every avenue to find a solution
🎻
instrument
直覺原意：樂器、儀器
抽象轉義：促成某事的手段、傀儡、工具
搭配詞：an instrument of dark forces / financial instrument
🪞
mirror
直覺原意：鏡子
抽象轉義：反映、折射出 (現實、狀況) (動詞)
搭配詞：the stock market mirrors the economy
🧶
fabric
直覺原意：布料、織物
抽象轉義：(社會、組織的) 基本結構、組成骨架
搭配詞：the fabric of society / the social fabric
🛏️
blanket
直覺原意：毛毯
抽象轉義：全面性的、一刀切的 (形容詞)
搭配詞：a blanket ban on smoking / blanket insurance
🧵
thread
直覺原意：一條線、縫線
抽象轉義：(對話、思路的) 思緒、主線、脈絡
搭配詞：lose the thread of the conversation
🫱
elbow
直覺原意：手肘
抽象轉義：擠進 (某圈子)、推開、強行排擠 (動詞)
搭配詞：elbow one's way into the market / elbowed out of the company
🧠
head
直覺原意：頭
抽象轉義：主管、率領 (動詞)；朝某方向前進 (動詞)
搭配詞：head the project / head towards the exit
🏋️
shoulder
直覺原意：肩膀
抽象轉義：承擔 (責任、費用、重擔) (動詞)
搭配詞：shoulder the responsibility / shoulder the blame
🦶
foot
直覺原意：腳
抽象轉義：買單、支付 (昂貴的費用) (動詞)
搭配詞：foot the bill / foot the cost
🔙
back
直覺原意：背部
抽象轉義：支持、做後盾、資助 (動詞)
搭配詞：the proposal was backed by the government
💪
muscle
直覺原意：肌肉
抽象轉義：強行介入 (動詞)；實力、影響力 (名詞)
搭配詞：muscle in on someone's territory / financial muscle
🍽️
stomach
直覺原意：胃、肚子
抽象轉義：忍受、承受得住 (令人不快的事) (動詞)
搭配詞：cannot stomach his arrogant attitude
👆
finger
直覺原意：手指
抽象轉義：準確指出、鎖定 (動詞)
搭配詞：put a finger on what's wrong / finger a suspect
👁️
eye
直覺原意：眼睛
抽象轉義：看中、審視、打量、計畫 (動詞)
搭配詞：eyeing expansion into Asia / eye the prize
🦴
backbone
直覺原意：脊椎骨
抽象轉義：骨幹、核心支柱、堅毅的意志
搭配詞：the backbone of the economy
⚓
harbor
直覺原意：港口
抽象轉義：心懷、潛伏 (怨恨、想法、病毒等) (動詞)
搭配詞：harbor a resentment / harbor doubts / harbor a virus
🌱
foster
直覺原意：領養、寄養
抽象轉義：培養、促進 (關係、技能、環境) (動詞)
搭配詞：foster a sense of community / foster growth
🪴
cultivate
直覺原意：耕作、種植
抽象轉義：培養 (品質、習慣)、建立發展 (關係) (動詞)
搭配詞：cultivate a habit of reading / cultivate relationships
⚖️
weigh
直覺原意：稱重量
抽象轉義：權衡、認真考慮 (利弊、重要性) (動詞)
搭配詞：weigh the pros and cons / weigh one's options
🪙
coin
直覺原意：硬幣
抽象轉義：創造、杜撰 (新詞彙、新說法) (動詞)
搭配詞：the term was coined in 1920. / coin a phrase
🚰
drain
直覺原意：排水管、消耗
抽象轉義：使枯竭、使精疲力竭、大舉消耗 (動詞)
搭配詞：drain the resource / brain drain / emotionally drained
🌐
domain
直覺原意：網域
抽象轉義：領域、勢力範圍、知識範疇
搭配詞：outside my domain of expertise / public domain
🌅
horizon
直覺原意：地平線
抽象轉義：眼界、見識、未來的發展前景
搭配詞：broaden one's horizons / on the horizon
🏞️
landscape
直覺原意：風景、景色
抽象轉義：(某種活動或形勢的) 全面形貌、格局
搭配詞：the political landscape / changing business landscape
🚧
boundary
直覺原意：邊界、國界
抽象轉義：(人際關係、道德、學術的) 分寸、界線
搭配詞：set personal boundaries / push the boundaries
🌦️
climate
直覺原意：氣候
抽象轉義：局勢、風氣、社會氛圍
搭配詞：the current economic climate / climate of fear
🌊
erosion
直覺原意：土壤流失、侵蝕
抽象轉義：(權力、價值、信任的) 削弱、逐漸瓦解
搭配詞：the erosion of trust
💨
evaporate
直覺原意：蒸發
抽象轉義：(希望、信心、財富) 憑空消失、幻滅 (動詞)
搭配詞：their hopes evaporated into thin air.
🙏
grace
直覺原意：優雅；(宗教) 恩典
抽象轉義 1：從容得體地應對逆境；寬限期 (名詞/動詞)
搭配詞：accept the loss with grace / a grace period
抽象轉義 2：使...增光、蒞臨、為...錦上添花 (及物動詞)
搭配詞：her beauty graced the cover of the magazine
💱
currency
直覺原意：貨幣
抽象轉義：普遍的認可度、流通程度、可信度
搭配詞：the idea gained currency among scholars
📶
bandwidth
直覺原意：(網路) 頻寬
抽象轉義：個人的心力、時間與精力額度
搭配詞：I don't have the bandwidth for another project
🛞
traction
直覺原意：(車輪的) 牽引力、抓地力
抽象轉義：獲得關注、支持度、進展
搭配詞：the campaign is gaining traction
😋
appetite
直覺原意：食慾
抽象轉義：渴望、興趣、意願 (常接 for)
搭配詞：a healthy appetite for risk
🧪
chemistry
直覺原意：化學 (科目)
抽象轉義：人與人之間的默契、投緣感
搭配詞：the two actors have great chemistry
👣
footprint
直覺原意：腳印
抽象轉義：影響範圍、佔用的資源或空間
搭配詞：reduce your carbon footprint
📫
address
字義 1・名詞
地址
mailing address / email address
字義 2・名詞
(正式) 演說、致詞
deliver a keynote address
字義 3・動詞
處理、解決 (問題)；向...發表演說
address the concerns raised by employees
🎁
present
字義 1・名詞
禮物
a birthday present
字義 2・形容詞
出席的；目前的、當下的
all members were present at the meeting
字義 3・動詞
呈現、提出、頒發
present the findings to the board
🏃
run
字義 1・動詞
跑步 (最基本的字義)
run five kilometers every morning
字義 2・動詞
經營、管理 (公司、店家)
run a small bakery
字義 3・動詞
(機器、程式) 運轉、持續進行
the software runs smoothly
字義 4・動詞
參選 (常接 for)
run for mayor

📝 實戰自我檢測 (高中/學測/多益風格)

1. Poetry has always been a powerful ______ of expression for writers to convey their deepest fears and joys.
2. We only have a narrow ______ of time to submit the university applications before the server closes tonight.
3. Small startups often find it extremely difficult to ______ their way into the market dominated by international tech giants.
4. The newly built national archives building will ______ over one million historical documents and ancient maps.
5. After carefully ______ the pros and cons of studying abroad, Jeremy finally decided to accept the scholarship from Tokyo University.
6. The high school English teacher encouraged her students to ______ a habit of reading English novels for at least 15 minutes every day.
7. When the rich businessman refused to pay for his son's expensive party, the boy's grandfather agreed to ______ the bill.
8. In today's highly competitive economic ______, online marketing has become crucial for any business to survive.
9. As the police provided more concrete evidence, all the suspect's lies quickly ______ into thin air.
10. Professor Wang is a world-renowned scientist who has spent over thirty years conducting ground-breaking research in the ______ of biotechnology.
11. Despite losing the final match, the young athlete accepted defeat with remarkable ______, congratulating her opponent warmly.
12. After the rumor started circulating online, the false claim quickly gained ______ among younger social media users.
13. As a working parent with three kids, I simply don't have the ______ to take on any more volunteer commitments this semester.
14. The new budget smartphone brand is slowly gaining ______ among price-conscious consumers in Southeast Asia.
15. Investors have shown little ______ for risky ventures since the market crash, preferring safer government bonds instead.
16. The mayor's opening speech will ______ concerns about the recent rise in local crime rates directly.
17. Despite tough competition, she decided to ______ for the position of student council president next year.
18. The research team will ______ their latest findings on renewable energy at the international conference next month.
